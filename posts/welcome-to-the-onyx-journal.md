We've spent the last several years sitting in regulatory pre-submission meetings, advising founders on capital strategy, and helping commercial teams translate clinical evidence into market-access narratives. Most of what we've learned never gets written down.

This is where we plan to change that.

## Why a journal, why now

The life sciences industry has more capital, more candidates, and more regulatory complexity than at any point in the last two decades. The frameworks for getting a product from concept to market exist, but they're scattered across regulators, payers, investor decks, and the heads of operators who've done it before. The result is that founders often discover late what they could have known early.

We don't believe the answer is more whitepapers. The answer is concrete, specific writing about the decisions we see teams getting right and wrong, and what we'd do differently next time.

## What we'll cover

The Journal will rotate across a few themes:

- **Regulatory strategy.** EMA scientific advice, GCC harmonization, and the pre-submission decisions that quietly determine your timeline.
- **Clinical development.** Trial design choices that pay off two years later, and the ones that don't.
- **Market access and pricing.** Reimbursement frameworks for the EU and GCC, and what payer logic actually rewards.
- **Capital and operations.** What investors look for at each stage, and how to build an organization that can actually execute the plan.

## How to read this

Posts will be short and opinionated. We'll try to publish things we'd say in a meeting, not things we'd say at a conference. If a post is wrong, we'll come back and fix it. If a post is interesting, send it to a colleague.

Welcome aboard.
